Features
- Customer registration and customer records
- Vehicle registration and fleet management
- Vehicle availability tracking
- Rental booking with date validation
- Vehicle return and late-return charges
- Rental payment records
- Vehicle maintenance status
- Rental history and revenue reports
- Input validation and error handling
- Separate Python modules for easier maintenance
